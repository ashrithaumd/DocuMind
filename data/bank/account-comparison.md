## Choosing an account

- If you're paid by direct deposit of at least $250 a month, Everyday
  Checking has no monthly fee.
- If you're a student, Student Checking has no monthly fee at all.
- For money you won't need for a while, a CD pays more than savings but
  charges a penalty if you withdraw early.
