This agreement is the contract between you and FinPilot Bank for your Cash
Back Card account. It includes the pricing table in Section 2, the Schedule
of Fees and the rate sheet. When you use the card or the account, or don't
cancel it within 30 days after we send you this agreement, you accept it.

"We", "us" and "our" mean FinPilot Bank. "You" and "your" mean the person
who applied for the account and is responsible for it. An **authorized
user** is someone you allow to use the account; authorized users are not
responsible for paying it.

## 1. Using your card

1.1 **What you can use it for.** You may use the card for purchases, balance
transfers and cash advances, for personal, family and household purposes.
You may not use it for illegal transactions or for business purposes.

1.2 **Credit limit.** We tell you your credit limit when we open the account,
and it appears on every statement. Part of it may be a separate cash
advance limit. We may approve a transaction that takes you over your limit,
but we don't have to, and we don't charge a fee if we do. You must pay any
amount over the limit.

1.3 **Changing your limit.** We may raise or lower your credit limit at any
time. We tell you when we lower it. You may ask for a higher limit in the
app; we may check your credit before we decide.

1.4 **Authorized users.** You may add authorized users in the app. You are
responsible for every transaction they make, even if they go beyond what you
allowed. To remove an authorized user, tell us in the app; we close their
card, and you remain responsible for what they charged before then.

1.5 **Declined transactions.** We may decline any transaction, for example
if we suspect fraud, if your account is past due, or if the transaction
would take you over your limit. We are not responsible if a merchant or ATM
refuses your card.

1.6 **Locking your card.** You can lock and unlock your card in the app.
While it is locked, purchases and cash advances are declined; recurring
payments you have set up with merchants may also be declined.

## 3. How your rates are set

3.1 **Prime rate.** Your APRs are the prime rate plus a margin. We use the
prime rate published in The Wall Street Journal on the last business day of
each month; a change takes effect on the first day of your next billing
cycle. On February 1, 2026 the prime rate was 6.50%.

3.2 **Margins.** We set your purchase margin when we open your account,
based on your creditworthiness, between 13.74 and 21.74 percentage points.
Your balance transfer margin is the same as your purchase margin. The cash
advance margin is 22.99 percentage points for everyone.

| Balance type | Margin over prime | APR on February 1, 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Purchases | 13.74 to 21.74 points | 20.24% to 28.24% |
| Balance transfers | 13.74 to 21.74 points | 20.24% to 28.24% |
| Cash advances | 22.99 points | 29.49% |

3.3 **When the prime rate changes.** If the prime rate goes up, your APRs go
up, your interest charges go up, and your minimum payment may go up. If it
goes down, they go down.

3.4 **Changes before February 1, 2026.** Until January 31, 2026, the prime
rate was 7.50%, purchase and balance transfer APRs were 21.24% to 29.24%,
and the cash advance APR was 29.99% (a margin of 22.49 points). The rate
sheet lists both versions.

## 4. Interest charges

4.1 **Grace period on purchases.** If you paid your previous statement's new
balance in full by its due date, we don't charge interest on new purchases
that appear on the current statement, as long as you also pay the current
statement's new balance in full by its due date. If you don't pay in full,
interest is charged on purchases from the date they post until they are paid.

4.2 **No grace period on cash advances and balance transfers.** Interest on
cash advances and balance transfers begins on the date the transaction
posts, whether or not you pay in full.

4.3 **How we calculate interest.** We use the average daily balance method,
including new transactions, for each balance type. For each day of the
billing cycle we take the beginning balance, add new transactions and fees,
subtract payments and credits, and treat a negative result as zero. We add
up the daily balances and divide by the number of days in the cycle to get
the average daily balance. Then we multiply the average daily balance by the
daily periodic rate (the APR divided by 365) and by the number of days in the
cycle.

4.4 **Example.** Your purchase APR is 24.24% and your average daily balance
for a 30-day billing cycle is $1,000.00. The interest charge is
$1,000.00 × (24.24% ÷ 365) × 30 = $19.92.

4.5 **Fees and interest.** Fees are added to your purchase balance and may
accrue interest at the purchase APR.

## 5. Fees

5.1 **Which fees apply.** We charge the fees in the pricing table and the
Schedule of Fees in force on the day a fee is assessed.

5.2 **Late payment fee.** If we don't receive at least the minimum payment by
the due date, we charge a late fee of up to $40.00. The fee is never more
than the minimum payment that was due. Before February 1, 2026, the late fee
was up to $39.00.

5.3 **Returned payment fee.** If a payment is returned unpaid, we charge up to
$29.00. The fee is never more than the minimum payment that was due.

5.4 **Foreign transaction fee.** We charge 3% of the U.S. dollar amount of
each transaction made outside the United States or in a foreign currency,
including online purchases from merchants abroad.

5.5 **Cash advance and balance transfer fees.** Each is 5% of the amount,
with a minimum of $10.00.

5.6 **Fees we don't charge.** There is no annual fee, no over-limit fee, no
penalty APR and no fee for a replacement card.

## 6. Payments

6.1 **Minimum payment.** Each month you must pay at least the minimum
payment by the due date. The minimum payment is the greater of $25.00 or 1%
of the new balance plus that month's interest and late fee, or the whole new
balance if it is less than $25.00. Any amount over your credit limit and any
past-due amount are added to the minimum payment.

6.2 **Paying more.** You may pay more than the minimum, up to the whole
balance, at any time, with no penalty. Paying only the minimum means you pay
more interest and take longer to repay your balance.

6.3 **How we apply payments.** We apply the minimum payment to balances as we
choose, usually starting with the lowest APR. We apply any amount above the
minimum to the balance with the highest APR first, then to the others in
order of APR.

6.4 **When payments are credited.** A payment made in the app or by phone
before 8:00 p.m. Eastern time on a business day is credited that day. A
payment by mail is credited on the day we receive it at the address on your
statement.

6.5 **Autopay.** You may set up automatic payments from a FinPilot Bank
checking account or another bank account for the minimum payment, the
statement balance or a fixed amount. If an automatic payment is returned
unpaid, the returned payment fee applies.

6.6 **Credit balances.** If you pay more than you owe, we apply the credit to
new transactions. You may ask for a refund of a credit balance at any time.

## 7. Cash back rewards

7.1 **Earning.** You earn 1.5% cash back on purchases, less returns and
credits. Balance transfers, cash advances, fees and interest don't earn cash
back.

7.2 **Redeeming.** Cash back is added to your rewards balance when the
purchase posts. You may redeem it in the app as a statement credit or a
deposit to a FinPilot Bank account, in any amount.

7.3 **Losing rewards.** You lose unredeemed cash back if we close your
account for fraud or misuse. If you close the account, we credit your
remaining cash back to the account first.

## 8. Statements, disputes and unauthorized use

8.1 **Statements.** We send a statement at the end of each billing cycle in
which there is a balance or activity. It shows your transactions, fees,
interest charges, minimum payment and due date.

8.2 **Billing errors and disputes.** To dispute a charge, a fee or interest,
or a payment that wasn't applied, tell us in writing within 60 days after we
sent the first statement showing the problem. Part B of the Dispute Policy
explains what happens next: we acknowledge your dispute within 30 days and
resolve it within two billing cycles, never more than 90 days. You don't
have to pay the disputed amount, or interest on it, while we investigate.

8.3 **Unauthorized use.** You are not responsible for charges you didn't
make or authorize. Tell us at once if your card is lost or stolen, or if you
see a charge you don't recognize; lock your card in the app while you
check.

8.4 **Problems with purchases.** If you have a problem with the quality of
goods or services you bought with the card, and you have tried in good
faith to fix it with the merchant, you may have the right not to pay the
remaining amount due on them, as the Dispute Policy and the law describe.

## 9. Default and closing

9.1 **Default.** You are in default if you don't make a minimum payment by
the due date, go over your credit limit, make a payment that is returned
unpaid, give us false information, file for bankruptcy, or die.

9.2 **What we may do.** If you are in default, we may decline transactions,
lower your credit limit, close your account, and, if you don't pay, ask you
to pay the whole balance at once. We don't charge a penalty APR.

9.3 **Closing by you.** You may close your account at any time in the app or
by phone. You must still pay the balance under the terms of this agreement.

9.4 **Closing by us.** We may close your account or suspend your card at any
time for any lawful reason. We tell you when we do.

9.5 **Credit reporting.** We report information about your account to
consumer reporting agencies. Late payments, missed payments or other
defaults may be reflected in your credit report. If you think we reported
something inaccurate, write to us at the address on your statement.

## 10. Changes and other terms

10.1 **Changes.** We may change this agreement, including the APRs and fees.
We give you at least 45 days' notice before a significant change, such as a
higher APR or a new fee, and tell you whether you can reject it. Changes in
the prime rate are not a change to this agreement.

10.2 **Notices.** We send notices to the email or mailing address in our
records, or in the app.

10.3 **Governing law.** This agreement is governed by federal law and, where
not preempted, the law of the state where you live when you open the
account.

10.4 **Severability and waiver.** If a court finds any term invalid, the
rest stays in force. If we don't enforce a term, or delay enforcing it, we
don't give up our right to enforce it later.

10.5 **Contact us.** Use the app (Help → Contact us), call the number on the
back of your card, or write to the address on your statement. Never send
your full card number or PIN by email or text.
