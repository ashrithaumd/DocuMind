# Deposit Account Agreement

> **DRAFT, pending review.** FinPilot Bank is a fictional bank created for a
> software project. Not a real institution; not FDIC-insured.

**Version:** 2025.2 · **Effective:** July 1, 2025 · **Applies to:** Everyday Checking, Student Checking, High-Yield Savings and certificates of deposit

This agreement, together with the Schedule of Fees, the rate sheet, the
Overdraft Policy and the Dispute Policy, is the contract between you and
FinPilot Bank for your deposit accounts. "We", "us" and "our" mean FinPilot
Bank; "you" and "your" mean each owner of the account.

## 1. Opening an account

1.1 To open an account we need your legal name, date of birth, address and a
government ID number, and we may check them against outside sources.

1.2 You must be 18 or older, except for Student Checking (17 or older).

1.3 We may decline to open an account for any lawful reason.

## 2. Deposits

2.1 **Funds availability.** Cash, direct deposits, wires and transfers from
another FinPilot Bank account are available on the business day we receive
them. The first $275 of a check deposit is available the next business day;
the rest by the second business day. We may hold larger deposits (over
$6,725 a day), deposits to accounts open less than 30 days, and redeposited
checks for up to 7 business days, and we tell you when we do.

2.2 **Mobile check deposits** made before 9:00 p.m. Eastern time on a
business day are received that day. Keep the paper check for 14 days, then
destroy it.

2.3 We may return a deposit that we can't collect, and reverse any credit we
gave for it.

## 3. Withdrawals and payments

3.1 You may withdraw your available balance at any time, subject to the
terms of a certificate of deposit.

3.2 **Available balance** is your balance minus pending debits and holds,
plus pending credits we have made available. We use the available balance to
decide whether a transaction overdraws your account.

3.3 Daily limits: ATM withdrawals $1,000; debit card purchases $5,000. We may
change these limits to protect your account.

## 4. Interest

4.1 Interest on interest-bearing accounts is compounded daily and credited
monthly, on the daily balance method.

4.2 Rates on checking and savings are **variable** and may change at any
time, without notice, unless the law requires it. The current rates are on
the rate sheet.

4.3 CD rates are fixed for the term. A CD renews automatically for the same
term at the rate then in effect unless you withdraw it within 10 days after
it matures (the grace period).

4.4 **Early withdrawal penalty for CDs:** 90 days of interest for a 6-month
term, 180 days for 12 months, 365 days for 24 months. The penalty may reduce
principal.

## 5. Fees

5.1 We charge the fees in the Schedule of Fees in force on the day a fee is
assessed. We may deduct fees from your account.

5.2 We give you at least **30 days' notice** before we raise a fee or add a
new one. Lowering or removing a fee needs no notice.

## 6. Statements and errors

6.1 We provide a statement each month for checking, and at least quarterly
for savings (monthly in any month with an electronic transfer).

6.2 Review each statement promptly. Report errors as set out in the Dispute
Policy, within 60 days of the statement date.

## 7. Protecting your account

7.1 Keep your card, PIN and sign-in details secure, and tell us at once if
they may have been lost, stolen or used without your permission.

7.2 We will **never** ask for your full card number, PIN, password or a
one-time passcode by phone, email or text. Anyone who does is not us.

7.3 We may freeze your account, hold a transaction, or decline a transaction
if we suspect fraud or unauthorized use, and we tell you as soon as we can
unless the law prevents us.

## 8. Closing an account

8.1 You may close your account at any time. We may close it for any lawful
reason, including an overdraft unpaid for 45 days, and send you any balance.

8.2 An account with no customer-initiated activity for 24 months is
dormant. After the period set by state law, we must send the balance to the
state as unclaimed property.

## 9. Changes and notices

9.1 We may change this agreement. We tell you at least 30 days before a
change that is unfavorable to you takes effect, unless the law allows a
shorter period.

9.2 Notices are sent to the email or mailing address in our records, or
delivered in the app.

## 10. Disputes and governing law

10.1 Please contact us first; most problems are resolved quickly. This
agreement is governed by federal law and, where not preempted, the law of
the state where the account was opened.
