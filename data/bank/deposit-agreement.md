# Deposit Account Agreement

> software project. Not a real institution; not FDIC-insured.

**Version:** 2025.2 · **Effective:** July 1, 2025 · **Applies to:** Everyday Checking, Student Checking, High-Yield Savings and certificates of deposit

This agreement, together with the Schedule of Fees, the rate sheet, the
Overdraft Policy and the Dispute Policy, is the contract between you and
FinPilot Bank for your deposit accounts. Please read it and keep it. When you
open an account, sign a signature card, accept these terms in the app, or
keep using your account after we tell you about a change, you agree to this
agreement as it then reads.

If this agreement and another document we give you disagree, the document
written specifically for your account (for example, a certificate of deposit
receipt) controls for that account, and this agreement controls for
everything else. If the law requires a different result, the law controls.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 **"We", "us" and "our"** mean FinPilot Bank. **"You" and "your"** mean
each owner of the account and anyone else with authority to act on it, such
as an agent under a power of attorney.

1.2 **"Account"** means each checking, savings or certificate of deposit
account you hold with us under this agreement.

1.3 **"Business day"** means Monday through Friday, except federal holidays.
Saturdays, Sundays and federal holidays are not business days, even if a
branch or the app is available.

1.4 **"Available balance"** means the money in your account that you can use
now. Section 7 explains how we work it out.

1.5 **"Ledger balance"** means the balance of all transactions that have
posted to your account, without counting pending transactions or holds.

1.6 **"Item"** means any order to pay money from or into your account: a
check, a debit card transaction, an ATM withdrawal, an electronic payment
(ACH), a wire, a transfer between accounts, a fee or any similar order.

1.7 **"Electronic fund transfer"** means a transfer started through an
electronic device or network, such as a debit card purchase, an ATM
withdrawal, a direct deposit, an online or mobile transfer, or a
preauthorized payment.

1.8 **"Schedule of Fees"** means our current schedule of fees for personal
accounts, which we may change under Section 20. The **rate sheet** means our
published table of interest rates and annual percentage yields (APYs).

1.9 **"App"** means FinPilot Bank's mobile and online banking services.

## 2. Opening an account

2.1 **Information we need.** Federal law requires us to get, verify and
record information that identifies each person who opens an account. To open
an account we need your legal name, date of birth, residential address and a
government identification number, and we may ask to see a government-issued
photo ID. We may check this information against outside sources, including
consumer reporting agencies that report on deposit account history.

2.2 **Age.** You must be 18 or older to open an account, except Student
Checking, which you may open at 17 or older. Student Checking is for students
aged 17 to 24 with a school email address.

2.5 **Tax reporting.** You certify that the taxpayer identification number
you give us is correct. If you don't give us a correct number, or the

## 3. Ownership of accounts

3.1 **Individual accounts.** An individual account is owned by one person.
Only that person, or someone they have authorized under Section 3.5, may act
on it.

3.2 **Joint accounts.** A joint account is owned by two or more people. Each
owner may deposit, withdraw all or part of the balance, close the account,
and give us instructions without the other owners' consent, and each owner is
responsible for every overdraft and fee on the account, whoever caused it.
Unless the law requires otherwise, when one owner dies the balance belongs to
the surviving owners. We may act on the instructions of any one owner, and a
notice we send to one owner is notice to all.

3.3 **Payable-on-death beneficiaries.** You may name one or more
beneficiaries on an individual or joint account. A beneficiary has no rights
to the account while any owner is alive. When the last owner dies, we pay the
balance to the beneficiaries who are then living, in equal shares unless your
instructions say otherwise. You may change or remove beneficiaries at any
time in writing or in the app.

3.4 **Minors.** A Student Checking owner who is 17 is bound by this
agreement as if they were an adult, to the extent the law allows. We may ask
a parent or guardian to be a joint owner.

3.5 **Powers of attorney and agents.** We may accept a power of attorney that
appears valid to us, and we may ask for a certification or legal opinion
first. We may refuse a power of attorney when the law allows us to. An agent
acts for you, and you are responsible for what your agent does with your
account until we receive written notice that the authority has ended and have
had a reasonable time to act on it.

## 4. Deposits

4.1 **Accepting deposits.** We may accept, refuse or return all or part of a
deposit. We credit deposits subject to final payment: if an item you deposit
is returned unpaid, we may reverse the credit and charge your account for the
amount, even if that overdraws it.

4.2 **Funds availability.** Our policy is to make funds from your deposits
available to you as set out below. "Day" means business day, and the day we
receive a deposit is day 0.

| Kind of deposit | When the money is available |
|---|---|
| Cash deposited at a FinPilot Bank ATM or branch | The business day we receive it |
| Direct deposits and other incoming electronic payments | The business day we receive them |
| Incoming wire transfers | The business day we receive them |
| Transfers from another FinPilot Bank account | Immediately |
| Checks: the first $275 of the day's check deposits | The next business day |
| Checks: the rest of the day's check deposits | The second business day |

4.3 **Longer holds.** We may hold check deposits longer, for up to 7
business days, when: you deposit checks totaling more than $6,725 on any one
day; you redeposit a check that was returned unpaid; your account has been
overdrawn repeatedly in the last six months; we have reasonable cause to
doubt that a check will be paid; or there is an emergency, such as a failure
of communications or computer equipment. If we hold a deposit longer, we tell
you when we do, and say when the money will be available.

4.4 **New accounts.** During the first 30 days after you open an account,
cash, direct deposits and wires are available on the business day we receive
them, the first $6,725 of a day's check deposits is available by the fifth
business day, and the rest by the ninth business day.

4.5 **Cut-off times.** A deposit made at a branch, at a FinPilot Bank ATM or
in the app before 9:00 p.m. Eastern time on a business day is received that
day. A deposit made after that time, or on a day that is not a business day,
is received on the next business day.

4.6 **Endorsements.** Checks you deposit must be endorsed on the back, within
the space marked for endorsement, by every payee. We may accept a check
without your endorsement and endorse it for you. You are responsible for any
loss caused by an endorsement that doesn't follow these rules.

4.7 **Foreign items.** We may refuse checks drawn on banks outside the United
States or payable in a foreign currency. If we accept one, we credit it only
after we collect it, at the exchange rate we receive, less any charges other
banks make.

## 5. Mobile check deposit

5.1 **Eligible checks.** You may deposit checks payable to you and drawn on a
U.S. bank by taking pictures of them in the app. You may not use mobile
deposit for checks payable to someone else, checks that are altered, checks
more than six months old, savings bonds, or checks you have already
deposited.

5.2 **Endorsement.** Before you take the pictures, endorse the check and
write "For mobile deposit only at FinPilot Bank" under your signature.

5.3 **Receipt.** A mobile deposit made before 9:00 p.m. Eastern time on a
business day is received that day. Our confirmation that we received the
pictures doesn't mean the deposit is accepted; we may review it and reject
it.

5.4 **Keeping the paper check.** Keep the paper check in a safe place for 14
days after the deposit, then destroy it. Don't deposit it again anywhere. If
the same check is paid twice, you must repay us.

5.5 **Limits.** We may set limits on the amount and number of mobile
deposits, and change them at any time. The app shows your current limits.

## 6. Withdrawals and payments

6.1 **Your right to withdraw.** You may withdraw your available balance at
any time, subject to the terms of a certificate of deposit (Section 10) and
to any hold, freeze or legal order (Section 16).

6.2 **Notice of withdrawal.** For savings accounts, federal rules let us
require seven days' written notice before a withdrawal. We don't require this

6.3 **Daily limits.** The following limits apply to each account unless we

| Kind of transaction | Daily limit |
|---|---|
| ATM cash withdrawals | $1,000 |
| Debit card purchases | $5,000 |
| Transfers to other people in the app | As shown in the app |

6.4 **Changing limits.** We may change these limits, temporarily or

6.5 **Checks you write.** We may pay a check without asking whether it is

the future until its date, but if we pay it early we are not responsible
unless you told us about it in a way that gave us a reasonable chance to act.

6.6 **Stop payments on checks.** You may ask us to stop payment on a check
that has not been paid. Tell us the check number, amount, date and payee. A
stop payment order lasts six months and may be renewed. The Schedule of Fees
shows the fee.

6.7 **Large cash withdrawals.** We may ask for advance notice of a large cash

## 7. Available balance and the order of posting

7.1 **How we work out your available balance.** Your available balance is
your ledger balance, minus debit card authorizations and other pending debits
we know about, minus holds on deposits, plus pending credits we have already
made available to you.

7.2 **Why it matters.** We use your available balance, not your ledger
balance, to decide whether you have enough money to pay an item, and whether
an item overdraws your account. Pending transactions can change your
available balance before they post.

7.3 **Authorizations.** When a merchant asks us to authorize a debit card
transaction, we hold the authorized amount until the transaction posts or
the hold ends, usually within 3 business days. Some merchants, such as hotels
and fuel stations, ask for an amount that differs from the final one; the
hold reflects the authorized amount until the transaction posts.

7.4 **Order of posting.** At the end of each business day we post credits
first, then debits, in the order described in the Overdraft Policy in force
on that day. The order in which we post items can affect whether an item
overdraws your account and whether a fee applies.

## 8. Electronic fund transfers

8.1 **Types of transfers.** You may use your debit card and the app to make
purchases, withdraw cash at ATMs, transfer money between your accounts, send

8.2 **Preauthorized payments.** If you have told a company to take regular

8.3 **Notice of direct deposits.** If you have arranged for direct deposits

8.4 **Documentation.** You can get a receipt at the time you make an ATM

statement.

8.5 **Our liability.** If we don't complete a transfer on time or in the
right amount, we are liable for your losses or damages, except when: you
don't have enough available balance; the ATM doesn't have enough cash; a
system was not working and you knew it when you started the transfer;
circumstances beyond our control prevented the transfer despite reasonable
precautions; the money is subject to legal process; or another exception in
this agreement applies.

8.6 **Errors and unauthorized transfers.** Your liability for unauthorized
electronic fund transfers, and how to report an error, are set out in the
Dispute Policy. In summary: if you tell us within 2 business days after you
learn that your card or sign-in details were lost or stolen, you can lose no
more than $50; after that, and up to 60 days after the statement showing the
first unauthorized transfer, you could lose up to $500. Our Zero Liability
promise for debit card purchases is described in the Dispute Policy.

8.7 **Disclosure of information to third parties.** We disclose information
about your account or the transfers you make to third parties only when it is
necessary to complete a transfer, to verify that your account exists and is
in good standing, to comply with a government order or the law, or with your
permission.

## 9. Interest

9.1 **Which accounts earn interest.** Everyday Checking, High-Yield Savings
and certificates of deposit earn interest. Student Checking does not.

9.2 **How we calculate interest.** We use the daily balance method, which
applies a daily periodic rate to the principal in the account each day. The
daily periodic rate is the interest rate divided by 365 (366 in a leap year).
Interest is compounded daily and credited monthly, on the last business day
of the statement period.

9.3 **When interest begins.** Interest begins to accrue on the business day
we receive credit for a deposit of cash or a check.

9.4 **Variable rates.** Rates on Everyday Checking and High-Yield Savings are
**variable** and may change at any time, without notice, unless the law
requires notice. The current rates and APYs are on the rate sheet. For
High-Yield Savings, the rate that applies to your whole balance on a given
day is the rate for the tier that balance falls in.

9.5 **Example.** If you keep $10,000.00 in High-Yield Savings for a 30-day
month at an interest rate of 3.54%, compounded daily, you earn about $29.14
that month. The figure is an example only; your interest depends on your
daily balances and the rate in force each day.

9.6 **Closing before interest is credited.** If you close an
interest-bearing account before interest is credited, we pay the interest
accrued to the day you close it.

## 10. Certificates of deposit

10.1 **Terms.** We offer certificates of deposit (CDs) with terms of 6, 12 and
24 months. The minimum deposit is $500.00. Your CD receipt shows the amount,
the term, the interest rate, the APY and the maturity date.

10.2 **Fixed rate.** The interest rate on a CD is fixed for its term.
Interest is compounded daily and credited monthly. You may have interest paid
into the CD, or transferred monthly to another FinPilot Bank account. The APY
assumes interest stays in the CD until maturity; withdrawing interest reduces
your earnings.

10.3 **Deposits during the term.** You may not add money to a CD during its
term, except during a grace period.

10.4 **Automatic renewal.** A CD renews automatically at maturity for the
same term, at the rate in effect for that term on the maturity date, unless
you withdraw it or change it during the grace period. The **grace period** is
the 10 calendar days after maturity. During the grace period you may withdraw
the money, add to it, or change the term, without penalty. We remind you at
least 20 days before a CD matures.

10.5 **Early withdrawal penalty.** If you withdraw principal before maturity,
we charge the penalty below. The penalty is calculated on the amount
withdrawn, at the CD's interest rate, simple interest.

| CD term | Early withdrawal penalty |
|---|---|
| 6 months | 90 days of interest |
| 12 months | 180 days of interest |
| 24 months | 365 days of interest |

The penalty may reduce your principal if you withdraw before enough interest
has been earned to cover it. After an early withdrawal, the rest of the CD
must stay at or above the minimum deposit, or we may close it and pay you
the balance less the penalty.

10.6 **Penalty example.** You deposit $5,000.00 in a 12-month CD at an
interest rate of 4.07% and withdraw it all after four months. The penalty is
180 days of simple interest: $5,000.00 × 4.07% × 180 ÷ 365 = $100.36. This
is more than the interest earned in four months, so part of the penalty
comes from principal.

10.7 **Exceptions.** We waive the early withdrawal penalty if an owner dies or
is declared legally incompetent by a court, when the law allows us to.

## 11. Fees

11.1 **Which fees apply.** We charge the fees in the Schedule of Fees in force
on the day a fee is assessed. A fee assessed before a new schedule takes
effect follows the earlier schedule, even if it appears on a statement
issued after the change.

11.2 **How we collect fees.** We may deduct fees from your account, even if
that overdraws it. Each fee appears as its own line on your statement, with
its date and the transaction it relates to, if any.

11.3 **Notice of changes.** We give you at least **30 days' notice** before we
raise a fee or add a new one. We may lower or remove a fee without notice.

11.4 **Fees charged by others.** Other banks, ATM owners and networks may
charge their own fees. We don't control those fees and don't refund them,
except where a product says otherwise (for example, the ATM reimbursements
described in the account comparison for Student Checking).

## 12. Overdrafts and returned items

12.1 **No obligation to pay.** We are not required to pay an item when your
available balance isn't enough. Whether we pay or return it is at our
discretion, except where the Overdraft Policy says we always decline.

12.2 **Overdraft Policy.** The Overdraft Policy in force on the day an item
posts governs which items we may pay into an overdraft, the fees, any
cushion before a fee applies, the daily limit on fees, and how you can opt in
to or out of overdraft coverage for ATM and everyday debit card transactions.

12.3 **Repaying overdrafts.** An overdraft is a debt you owe us, due at once.
If your account stays overdrawn, we may use deposits you make to repay it,
use our right of setoff (Section 16.4), close the account (Section 19), and
report the unpaid amount to a consumer reporting agency.

12.4 **Returned items.** If we return an item unpaid, the person you paid may
charge you a fee. Any fee we charge for a returned item is in the Schedule of
Fees in force on that day.

## 13. Statements, notices and electronic communications

13.1 **Statements.** We provide a statement each month for checking. For
savings, we provide a statement at least each quarter, and monthly in any
month with an electronic fund transfer. CD owners receive a statement at
least once a year and a maturity notice before each maturity.

13.2 **Electronic statements.** If you choose electronic statements, we post
them in the app and tell you by email when one is ready. A statement is
delivered on the day we post it. Paper statements may carry a fee under the
Schedule of Fees.

13.3 **Electronic communications.** By agreeing to electronic delivery you
consent to receive this agreement, changes to it, statements, notices and
other disclosures electronically. You may withdraw that consent at any time
in the app or by contacting us; your paper statements may then carry a fee.
Keep your email address up to date.

13.4 **Your duty to review statements.** Review each statement as soon as
you receive it. You must report errors as set out in the Dispute Policy,
within **60 days** of the statement date. For problems with checks, such as
a forged signature or an alteration, you must tell us within 60 days of the
statement date too, or you may lose your right to have us pay you back for
that item and any later item by the same wrongdoer.

13.5 **Address changes.** Tell us promptly when your address, email or phone
number changes. A notice we send to the most recent address in our records
is effective even if you don't receive it.

## 14. Errors and disputes about your account

14.1 **The Dispute Policy.** How to report an error or an unauthorized
transaction, what we do next, and how long it takes are set out in the
Dispute Policy, which is part of this agreement.

14.2 **Fees charged in error.** If you think we charged a fee in error,

14.3 **Provisional credit.** When the Dispute Policy says we provisionally
credit your account during an investigation, you may use that money. If we
find no error, we reverse the credit after giving you notice.

## 15. Protecting your account

15.1 **Your responsibilities.** Keep your card, PIN, password and app sign-in
details secure. Don't write your PIN on your card or share it. Tell us at
once if any of them may have been lost, stolen or used without your
permission, or if you see a transaction you don't recognize.

15.2 **What we will never ask.** We will **never** ask for your full card
number, PIN, password or a one-time passcode by phone, email or text. Anyone
who does is not us. Hang up, and contact us using the number on your card or
in the app.

15.3 **Fraud prevention.** We may freeze your account, hold a transaction, or
decline a transaction if we suspect fraud or unauthorized use, and we tell
you as soon as we can unless the law prevents us. We may ask you to confirm a
transaction through the app before we release it.

15.4 **Scams.** Be cautious when someone you don't know asks you to send
money, buy gift cards, or move money to "protect" it. A payment you
authorize, even one you were tricked into making, may not be an unauthorized
transfer, and we may be unable to recover it. Contact us at once if you think
you were scammed.

15.5 **Checks.** Keep your checks in a safe place, and tell us right away if
any are missing. You must take reasonable care to prevent forgery and
alterations.

## 16. Holds, freezes, legal process and setoff

16.1 **Holds and freezes.** We may place a hold on or freeze your account if
there is a dispute about who owns it or who may use it, if we suspect fraud
or illegal activity, if we receive conflicting instructions from joint
owners, or if the law requires it.

16.2 **Legal process.** If we receive a levy, garnishment, subpoena or other
legal order about your account, we may hold and pay out money as the order
requires, and we may charge your account for our costs as the law allows.
Certain federal benefits deposited directly into your account are protected
from garnishment as federal law provides.

16.3 **Adverse claims.** If someone claims an interest in your account, we
may refuse to pay the money until the claim is settled or a court orders us
to pay.

16.4 **Setoff.** If you owe us money that is due, we may take money from any
account you own, alone or jointly, to pay the debt, unless the law prohibits
it. We do not use setoff against a deposit account to collect a debt on a
credit card, and we do not set off federal benefits protected by law.

## 17. Death or incapacity

17.1 **Notice.** Please tell us promptly if an owner dies or is declared
legally incompetent. Until we receive notice and have had a reasonable time to
act on it, we may continue to pay items and accept deposits.

17.2 **What we may require.** We may ask for a death certificate, letters of
administration, a court order or other documents before we release money. We
may hold the account until we receive them.

17.3 **Paying the balance.** For a joint account, we pay the balance to the
surviving owners. For an account with payable-on-death beneficiaries and no
surviving owner, we pay the beneficiaries (Section 3.3). Otherwise we pay the
owner's estate. We may return any deposits made after the owner's death to
the person who made them.

## 18. Dormant accounts and unclaimed property

18.1 **Dormant accounts.** An account with no customer-initiated activity for
24 months is dormant. Interest continues to accrue on an interest-bearing
dormant account. We try to contact you before an account becomes dormant.

18.2 **Unclaimed property.** After the period set by the law of the state of
your last known address, we must send the balance of a dormant account to
that state as unclaimed property. After that, you must claim the money from
the state.

## 19. Closing an account

19.1 **Closing by you.** You may close your account at any time in the app,
by phone or in writing. We may keep the account open long enough to pay
items that are outstanding or pending, and we pay you the remaining balance.

19.2 **Closing by us.** We may close your account at any time for any lawful
reason, including an overdraft unpaid for 45 days, suspected fraud, abusive
conduct toward our staff, or if you give us false information. We may close
it without notice when the law allows. We send you the remaining balance by
check or transfer, less what you owe us.

19.3 **After closing.** We may refuse items presented after the account is
closed, and we are not liable for refusing them. This agreement continues to
apply to anything that happened before the account closed.

22.2 **Circumstances beyond our control.** We are not liable for delays or

22.4 **Governing law.** This agreement is governed by federal law and, where
