# Dispute and Error Resolution Policy

> **DRAFT, pending review.** FinPilot Bank is a fictional bank created for a
> software project. Not a real institution; not FDIC-insured.

**Version:** 2025 · **Effective:** January 1, 2025 · **Applies to:** checking, savings, debit cards and the Cash Back Card

This policy explains how to tell us about a mistake or an unauthorized
transaction, what we do next, and how long it takes. Debit card and other
electronic transfers from your deposit accounts follow Part A. Charges on
your Cash Back Card follow Part B.

## Part A. Deposit accounts and debit cards

### A1. What you can dispute

- A transfer or card purchase you didn't make or authorize.
- A transaction for the wrong amount, or posted twice.
- A deposit or transfer that's missing from your statement.
- A fee you believe we charged in error, including an overdraft fee.
- A math or posting error by us.

### A2. How to tell us

Contact us in the app (Help → Report a problem), by phone, by secure message,
or in writing at the address on your statement. Tell us:

1. your name and account number (the last four digits are enough in the app);
2. the transaction you're disputing, its date and amount;
3. why you believe it's an error, or why you need more information.

### A3. Deadlines for you

- **Unauthorized use of a lost or stolen card:** tell us as soon as you can.
  If you tell us within **2 business days** of learning of the loss, you can
  lose no more than **$50**. After that, and up to 60 days after the
  statement showing the first unauthorized transaction, you could lose up to
  **$500**. After 60 days, you could lose what was taken after the 60 days
  and before you told us.
- **Any other error:** tell us within **60 days** after we send the first
  statement on which the problem appeared.

FinPilot Bank's **Zero Liability promise**: for debit card purchases you
didn't authorize, we waive the limits above when you report them within 60
days of the statement date, unless we can show you were grossly negligent or
took part in the fraud.

### A4. What we do and how long it takes

| Step | Timeline |
|---|---|
| We confirm we received your dispute | Within 1 business day in the app; 3 business days otherwise |
| We investigate and tell you the result | Within **10 business days** |
| If we need more time | Up to **45 days** (90 days for new accounts, point-of-sale and foreign transactions). In that case we provisionally credit your account within 10 business days, so you have use of the money while we finish. |
| We tell you the result | Within 3 business days after we finish |

If we find no error, we explain why in writing, reverse any provisional
credit (with 5 business days' notice), and send you the documents we relied
on if you ask.

## Part B. Cash Back Card (credit card)

### B1. What you can dispute

- A charge you didn't make or authorize, or for the wrong amount.
- A charge for goods or services you didn't accept or that weren't delivered
  as agreed.
- A payment or credit that wasn't applied.
- A fee or interest charge you believe is wrong.

### B2. How and when to tell us

Tell us **in writing** (secure message in the app counts) within **60 days**
after we sent the first statement showing the problem. You don't have to pay
the disputed amount, or interest on it, while we investigate; you still have
to pay the rest of your bill.

### B3. What we do and how long it takes

- We acknowledge your dispute within **30 days** of receiving it.
- We resolve it within **two billing cycles**, and never more than **90
  days**.
- While we investigate, we don't report the disputed amount as late or try to
  collect it.

### B4. Unauthorized use

You are not responsible for unauthorized charges on your Cash Back Card.

## Part C. Disputing a fee

To dispute a fee, give us the fee's date and amount. We check it against the
fee schedule that was in force on the day the fee was assessed (the 2025
schedule before February 1, 2026; the 2026 schedule from then on). If the fee
didn't follow that schedule, we refund it. We may also refund a fee as a
courtesy; a courtesy refund doesn't mean the fee was charged in error.
