# FinPilot Bank Schedule of Fees: Personal Accounts

> **DRAFT, pending review.** FinPilot Bank is a fictional bank created for a
> software project. Not a real institution; not FDIC-insured.

**Version:** 2025 · **Effective:** January 1, 2025 · **Replaced by:** the 2026 schedule on February 1, 2026

This schedule lists the fees we may charge on personal checking, savings and
credit card accounts. It is part of your Deposit Agreement (for deposit
accounts) and your Cardmember Agreement (for credit cards). Where a fee
depends on how you use your account, the condition is in the same row.

## 1. Everyday Checking

| Service | Fee | When it applies |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly maintenance | $12.00 | Charged on the last day of each statement month. **Waived** in any month in which direct deposits to the account total $500.00 or more. Always waived for Student Checking. |
| Overdraft (paid item) | $34.00 per item | When we pay a transaction that overdraws your available balance. See the Overdraft Policy (2025). |
| Overdraft fee limit | 3 per day | We charge no more than 3 overdraft fees on any business day. |
| Returned item (NSF) | $34.00 per item | When we return a check or electronic payment unpaid because your available balance is too low. |
| Stop payment | $30.00 | Per request, by phone, online or in a branch. |
| Paper statement | $2.00 per statement | Not charged if you choose electronic statements. |

## 2. ATM and debit card

| Service | Fee | When it applies |
|---|---|---|
| FinPilot Bank ATM | No fee | — |
| Out-of-network ATM withdrawal | $3.00 | Per withdrawal at an ATM we don't own. The ATM owner may charge its own fee too. |
| Out-of-network balance inquiry | $1.50 | — |
| Foreign transaction (debit card) | 3% of the U.S. dollar amount | Purchases and withdrawals processed outside the United States or in a foreign currency. |
| Replacement debit card | $5.00 | Standard delivery. **$25.00** for expedited delivery. No fee if the card was stolen or compromised. |

## 3. Savings

| Service | Fee | When it applies |
|---|---|---|
| High-Yield Savings monthly fee | No fee | — |
| Transfer to checking | No fee | Online, mobile and phone transfers. |

## 4. Payments and wires

| Service | Fee | When it applies |
|---|---|---|
| Outgoing domestic wire | $25.00 | Per wire. |
| Outgoing international wire | $45.00 | Per wire. Intermediary banks may deduct their own charges. |
| Incoming wire | $15.00 | Per wire. |
| Cashier's check | $10.00 | Per check. |

## 5. Cash Back Card (credit card)

| Service | Fee | When it applies |
|---|---|---|
| Annual fee | None | — |
| Late payment | Up to $39.00 | If we don't receive the minimum payment by the due date. Never more than the minimum payment that was due. |
| Returned payment | Up to $29.00 | If a payment is returned unpaid. |
| Foreign transaction | 3% of each transaction in U.S. dollars | — |
| Cash advance | 5% of the amount (minimum $10.00) | — |

Interest rates for the Cash Back Card are in the rate sheet.

## 6. How fees are shown

Every fee appears as its own line on your statement, with the date and the
transaction it relates to, if any. If you think a fee was charged in error,
contact us within 60 days of the statement date (see the Dispute Policy).
