# FinPilot Bank Schedule of Fees: Personal Accounts

> **DRAFT, pending review.** FinPilot Bank is a fictional bank created for a
> software project. Not a real institution; not FDIC-insured.

**Version:** 2026 · **Effective:** February 1, 2026 · **Replaces:** the 2025 schedule

This schedule lists the fees we may charge on personal checking, savings and
credit card accounts. It is part of your Deposit Agreement (for deposit
accounts) and your Cardmember Agreement (for credit cards).

## What changed on February 1, 2026

- **Overdraft fee lowered** from $34.00 to **$15.00** per item, and the daily
  limit from 3 fees to **2**.
- **No overdraft fee when you're overdrawn by $50.00 or less** after the
  transaction posts (the "overdraft cushion").
- **Returned item (NSF) fee eliminated.** We no longer charge when we return
  a check or payment unpaid.
- **Monthly maintenance fee lowered** from $12.00 to **$10.00**, and the
  direct-deposit amount that waives it from $500.00 to **$250.00** a month.
- **Out-of-network ATM fee raised** from $3.00 to **$3.50**.
- **Credit card late fee** is now up to **$40.00** (was up to $39.00).

## 1. Everyday Checking

| Service | Fee | When it applies |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly maintenance | $10.00 | Charged on the last day of each statement month. **Waived** in any month in which direct deposits to the account total $250.00 or more. Always waived for Student Checking. |
| Overdraft (paid item) | $15.00 per item | When we pay a transaction that overdraws your available balance by more than $50.00. See the Overdraft Policy (2026). |
| Overdraft cushion | $50.00 | No overdraft fee if your available balance is overdrawn by $50.00 or less after the transaction. |
| Overdraft fee limit | 2 per day | We charge no more than 2 overdraft fees on any business day. |
| Returned item (NSF) | No fee | — |
| Stop payment | $30.00 | Per request. |
| Paper statement | $3.00 per statement | Not charged if you choose electronic statements, or if you are 65 or older. |

## 5. Cash Back Card (credit card)

| Service | Fee | When it applies |
|---|---|---|
| Annual fee | None | — |
| Late payment | Up to $40.00 | If we don't receive the minimum payment by the due date. Never more than the minimum payment that was due. |
| Returned payment | Up to $29.00 | If a payment is returned unpaid. |
| Foreign transaction | 3% of each transaction in U.S. dollars | — |

Interest rates for the Cash Back Card are in the rate sheet.

## 6. How fees are shown

Every fee appears as its own line on your statement, with the date and the
transaction it relates to, if any. Fees are charged under the schedule in
force on the day the fee is assessed: a fee assessed on January 31, 2026 uses
the 2025 schedule, and one assessed on February 1, 2026 uses this one. If you
think a fee was charged in error, contact us within 60 days of the statement
date (see the Dispute Policy).
