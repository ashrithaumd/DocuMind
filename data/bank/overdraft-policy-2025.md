## 1. What an overdraft is

An overdraft happens when you don't have enough money in your account to
cover a transaction, and we pay it anyway. When we pay it, your balance goes
below zero and you owe us the difference. We may also decline or return the
transaction instead of paying it.

## 2. Which transactions we may pay

- **Checks, automatic bill payments and recurring debit card payments.** We
  may pay these into an overdraft at our discretion. Our decision depends on
  your account history, your deposits, and the amount of the overdraft.
- **ATM withdrawals and one-time (everyday) debit card purchases.** We pay
  these into an overdraft **only if you have opted in** to overdraft coverage
  for them. If you haven't, we decline them when your available balance is
  too low, and you pay no overdraft fee for a declined transaction.

You can opt in or out at any time in the mobile app, online, by phone or in a
branch. Opting out stops coverage for ATM and everyday debit card
transactions only.

## 3. Fees

| Situation | Fee |
|---|---|
| We pay an item that overdraws your account | $34.00 per item |
| We return an item unpaid (NSF) | $34.00 per item |
| Most fees we charge in one business day | 3 (overdraft and NSF combined) |

An overdraft fee is charged for each item we pay while your available balance
is below zero. There is no minimum overdraft amount: an item that overdraws
your account by $1.00 is charged the full $34.00 fee.

## 4. Order of posting

At the end of each business day we post credits first, then debits in this
order: (1) transactions we have already authorized, by the time they were
authorized; (2) checks, by check number; (3) other electronic payments, from
smallest to largest. The order can change how many items overdraw your
account.

## 5. Repaying an overdraft

You must bring your account back to a positive balance promptly. If your
account stays overdrawn for 30 days in a row, we may close it and report the
unpaid amount to a consumer reporting agency.

## 6. Ways to avoid overdrafts

- Turn on low-balance alerts in the app.
- Link your High-Yield Savings account for **overdraft transfers**: when your
  checking balance would go below zero, we move the amount needed from
  savings, with no transfer fee.
- Opt out of overdraft coverage for ATM and everyday debit card transactions.

## 7. Questions

Call us, write to us through secure messaging in the app, or visit a branch.
If you believe an overdraft or NSF fee was charged in error, see the Dispute
Policy.
