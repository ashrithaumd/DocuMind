# Overdraft Policy

> **DRAFT, pending review.** FinPilot Bank is a fictional bank created for a
> software project. Not a real institution; not FDIC-insured.

**Version:** 2026 · **Effective:** February 1, 2026 · **Replaces:** the 2025 policy · **Applies to:** Everyday Checking and Student Checking

## Summary of changes

- The overdraft fee is **$15.00** (was $34.00).
- **No fee if you're overdrawn by $50.00 or less** after the item posts.
- At most **2** overdraft fees a business day (was 3).
- **No fee for returned items** (NSF). Returned items no longer count toward
  the daily limit.

## 1. What an overdraft is

An overdraft happens when you don't have enough money in your account to
cover a transaction, and we pay it anyway. When we pay it, your balance goes
below zero and you owe us the difference. We may also decline or return the
transaction instead of paying it.

## 2. Which transactions we may pay

- **Checks, automatic bill payments and recurring debit card payments.** We
  may pay these into an overdraft at our discretion, based on your account
  history, your deposits, and the amount of the overdraft.
- **ATM withdrawals and one-time (everyday) debit card purchases.** We pay
  these into an overdraft **only if you have opted in** to overdraft coverage
  for them. If you haven't, we decline them when your available balance is
  too low, with no fee.
- **Declines at $200.** Whether or not you've opted in, we decline everyday
  debit card purchases once your account is overdrawn by more than $200.00.

## 3. Fees

| Situation | Fee |
|---|---|
| We pay an item and your available balance is overdrawn by **more than $50.00** afterwards | $15.00 per item |
| We pay an item and you are overdrawn by **$50.00 or less** afterwards | No fee |
| We return an item unpaid | No fee |
| Most overdraft fees in one business day | 2 |

**Example.** Your available balance is $20.00. A $60.00 purchase posts,
leaving you overdrawn by $40.00: no fee, because $40.00 is within the
cushion. A $25.00 payment posts the same day, leaving you overdrawn by
$65.00: one $15.00 fee. A third item that day is charged one more $15.00
fee; a fourth is charged nothing, because the daily limit is 2.

## 4. Order of posting

At the end of each business day we post credits first, then debits in this
order: (1) transactions we have already authorized, by the time they were
authorized; (2) checks, by check number; (3) other electronic payments, by
the time we received them. (Before February 1, 2026, electronic payments
posted from smallest to largest.)

## 5. Repaying an overdraft

You must bring your account back to a positive balance promptly. If your
account stays overdrawn for 45 days in a row (was 30), we may close it and
report the unpaid amount to a consumer reporting agency.

## 6. Ways to avoid overdrafts

- Turn on low-balance alerts in the app.
- Link your High-Yield Savings account for **overdraft transfers**, with no
  transfer fee.
- Opt out of overdraft coverage for ATM and everyday debit card transactions.

## 7. Fees assessed under the earlier policy

Overdraft and NSF fees assessed **before February 1, 2026** were charged
under the 2025 policy and are not refunded because of this change. If you
believe a fee was charged in error under either policy, see the Dispute
Policy.
