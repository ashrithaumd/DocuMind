Atlas mover — battery pack specification (rev. D)
Version 1.0, 2026.

Chemistry: lithium iron phosphate. Nominal 48 V, 105 Ah.
Runtime: 9 hours at a typical duty cycle of 60 percent.
Charging: 1.5 hours from 20 to 90 percent on the dock charger.
Cycle life: rated for 4,000 cycles to 80 percent of original capacity.
Operating temperature: -5 to 45 degrees Celsius. Below 0 degrees the pack
heats itself before charging.

Packs are hot-swappable in under 3 minutes by one technician. Replacement
packs are tracked by serial number in Skyview and must be recycled through
the CellCore take-back program.
