Aurora Robotics — Travel and Expense Policy
Owner: People team. Effective 1 March 2026. Applies to all employees.

1. Booking
Book flights and hotels through the TripDesk portal. Economy class is the
default. Business class is allowed only for flights longer than 8 hours,
and needs written approval from a director.

2. Meals
The daily meal allowance while travelling is 75 USD per person. Alcohol is
never reimbursed. Client dinners may exceed the allowance up to 120 USD per
attendee if the client's name is noted on the claim.

3. Hotels
The nightly hotel cap is 220 USD in most cities and 310 USD in New York,
London, Tokyo and Zurich.

4. Mileage
Personal car use is reimbursed at 0.62 USD per mile.

5. Submitting claims
Submit receipts in ExpenseHub within 30 days of the trip. Claims older than
90 days are not reimbursed. Any single expense above 500 USD needs a
receipt and your manager's approval before you submit.

Questions go to expenses@aurora-robotics.example.
