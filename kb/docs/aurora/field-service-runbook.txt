Field-ops — on-site service runbook

Response times by contract tier
- Platinum: technician on site within 4 hours, 24/7.
- Gold: within 8 business hours.
- Silver: next business day.

Before going on site, check the robot's last 24 hours of logs in Skyview
and bring a spare battery pack for any Atlas ticket.

Safety
Lock out the robot with the physical key before opening any panel. Two
technicians are required for any lift work above 1.5 m. Report every safety
stop you witness in the incident form within 2 hours.

Common fixes
- Pika gripper misses: recalibrate the depth cameras with the checkerboard
  target, which takes about 20 minutes.
- Atlas drifting off route: clean the lidar window, then rerun floor-map
  alignment from the service tablet.
- Robot will not dock: check the dock's charging contacts for debris; the
  contacts should read 48 to 54 V with a multimeter.

Close every ticket with the parts used and the time on site.
