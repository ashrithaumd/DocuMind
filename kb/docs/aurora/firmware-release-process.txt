Firmware release process (Pika and Atlas)
Owner: firmware team.

Releases ship every four weeks on a Tuesday. A release candidate is cut on
the Wednesday before, and must pass:
- the hardware-in-the-loop suite on 3 Pika and 2 Atlas units,
- a 48-hour soak on the test floor with at least 20 robots,
- a sign-off from field-ops for any change to motor control.

Rollout is staged: 5 percent of the fleet on day one, 25 percent on day
three, everyone by day seven. Any safety-stop rate above 0.2 per robot-hour
pauses the rollout automatically.

Version numbers follow YEAR.RELEASE.PATCH, for example 2026.11.0 for the
eleventh release of 2026. Hotfixes skip the soak but still need two
reviewers from the firmware team.
