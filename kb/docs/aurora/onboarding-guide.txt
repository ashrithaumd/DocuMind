Welcome to Aurora Robotics
Version 1.0, 2026.

Your first week
- Day 1: collect your badge from reception at the Pittsburgh office,
  Building C, before 9:30. IT hands out laptops in room C-114.
- Day 2: safety induction for the robot test floor. Nobody enters the test
  floor without it, including office staff.
- Day 3: meet your onboarding buddy, who is assigned by your manager.
- By the end of week one, complete the four compliance modules in LearnPath.

Tools you will use: Slack for chat, Linear for tickets, Notion for
documents, and the internal fleet dashboard "Skyview" for live robot data.

Equipment: every employee gets a laptop and one external monitor. A second
monitor can be requested after 90 days.

Probation is three months. Your manager holds a check-in at 30, 60 and 90
days.
