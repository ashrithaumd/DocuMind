Product spec — Pika picker, generation 3

Pika is our shelf-picking robot for small parcels.

Key numbers
- Maximum payload: 12 kg.
- Pick rate: up to 600 items per hour in a standard aisle layout.
- Reach: shelves from 0.2 m to 2.4 m high.
- Battery: 6 hours of continuous picking, 55 minutes to recharge to 80 percent.
- Navigation: lidar plus two depth cameras; no floor markers needed.

Generation 3 adds a suction gripper for polybags and raises the pick rate
from 450 to 600 items per hour. The target list price is 38,000 USD per
unit, with the fleet software billed separately.

Open questions: whether the 2.4 m reach is enough for European customers,
whose racking is often 2.7 m.
