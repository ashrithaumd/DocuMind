Postmortem — Atlas fleet stall at Brightmart Columbus, 19 August 2026
Version 1.0, 2026.
Severity: SEV1. Duration: 2 hours 14 minutes.

Summary
At 06:12 Eastern, 63 of the 140 Atlas movers at the Brightmart distribution
center in Columbus stopped and refused new missions. Picking throughput at
the site fell by about 45 percent until 08:26.

Root cause
A map update pushed at 06:05 contained a no-go zone drawn with an
inverted polygon, which covered most of the floor. The map validator only
checked that polygons were closed, not their orientation.

What went well
The on-call engineer acknowledged the page in 3 minutes, and the field-ops
lead on site confirmed the robots were stopped safely.

What went badly
Rolling back the map took 70 minutes because the previous map version had
been garbage-collected from the site cache.

Action items
1. Validate polygon orientation in the map validator. Owner: Mei Tanaka.
2. Keep the last 5 map versions in every site cache. Owner: Luis Ferreira.
3. Offer Brightmart a service credit of 15,000 USD. Owner: account team.
