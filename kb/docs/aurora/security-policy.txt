Aurora Robotics — Information Security Policy (summary)
Version 1.0, 2026.

- Passwords: at least 14 characters; the password manager is 1Password.
- Multi-factor authentication is required for every company system. Hardware
  keys are required for anyone with production access.
- Laptops lock after 5 minutes idle and use full-disk encryption.
- Production access is granted for 12 hours at a time through the access
  portal and is logged.
- Report a lost device within 1 hour to security@aurora-robotics.example.
- Customer floor maps are confidential and must never be shared outside
  the company without a signed data-sharing agreement.
- Security training is annual; new hires complete it in their first two
  weeks.
