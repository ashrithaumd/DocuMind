Summary of the CellCore battery supply agreement (confidential)

Supplier: CellCore Energy Ltd. Term: 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2028.
Price: 2,150 USD per Atlas pack for the first 1,000 packs each year, and
1,980 USD per pack above that volume.
Minimum annual order: 600 packs. Shortfall penalty: 12 percent of the
unmet volume's value.
Warranty: 3 years or 3,000 cycles, whichever comes first.
Take-back: CellCore collects and recycles end-of-life packs at no charge.
Either party may end the agreement with 9 months' notice after 1 January
2027.
