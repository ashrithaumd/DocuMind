Product spec — Current (demand forecasting), version 4

Current predicts daily unit demand per store and product, 8 weeks ahead.

- Refresh: forecasts recompute nightly at 02:00 Eastern.
- Granularity: store x product x day. Our largest customer has about 1.2
  billion such combinations.
- Accuracy target: WAPE under 28 percent at the store-product-week level.
- Inputs: point-of-sale history (minimum 2 years), promotions calendar,
  local weather, and public holidays.
- Version 4 adds promotion uplift modelling and drops the old ARIMA
  fallback.

Pricing is per store per month (see the pricing document). Customers can
export forecasts to their ERP via a daily CSV drop or the REST API.
