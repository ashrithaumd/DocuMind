Bluefin Analytics — Expenses
Version 1.0, 2026.

We keep this short.

- Meals while travelling: up to 60 USD a day. Team dinners: up to 45 USD
  per person, once a quarter, approved by your manager.
- Hotels: up to 190 USD a night; 260 USD in San Francisco and New York.
- Flights: economy only, booked at least 14 days ahead where possible.
- Home-office stipend: 600 USD once, plus 50 USD a month for internet.
- Claims go in Ramp within 45 days, with a photo of the receipt. Anything
  above 250 USD needs your manager's approval first.
- Conference tickets need approval from the finance team if they cost more
  than 1,000 USD.
