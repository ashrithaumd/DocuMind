Bluefin Analytics — onboarding

We are remote-first. Your laptop ships to your home address before your
start date; if it hasn't arrived 2 days before you start, write to
it-help@bluefin.example.

Week one
- Monday: welcome call at 11:00 Eastern with the People partner.
- Tuesday: set up your Snowflake and dbt access through the access request
  form; approval takes up to one business day.
- Wednesday: shadow a support shift, whatever your role, so you meet our
  customers' problems early.
- Friday: present one thing you learned in the #new-joiners channel.

Every new joiner gets an onboarding buddy and a 30-60-90 day plan from their
manager. Probation is 90 days.
