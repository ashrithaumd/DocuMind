Postmortem — stale forecasts for 11 customers, 22 July 2026
Version 1.0, 2026.
Severity: P1. Customer impact: 19 hours of stale forecasts.

What happened
The nightly forecast run at 02:00 Eastern failed for 11 customers, including
Northgate Grocers, our largest account. Dashboards kept showing the
previous day's forecasts until 21:00, and nobody was paged until 07:40,
when Northgate's team reported it.

Root cause
A schema change to the promotions table renamed the column promo_type to
promotion_type. The forecasting job read the old name and failed, and the
failure alert was routed to a Slack channel that had been archived.

Action items
1. Schema contracts on every table the forecasting job reads. Owner: data-eng.
2. Route the forecast-failure alert to PagerDuty, not Slack. Owner: ML lead.
3. Credit of 8,000 USD to Northgate Grocers. Owner: finance.
