Remote work at Bluefin

Everyone works remotely, from any country where we have a legal entity:
the United States, Canada and Portugal. Working from another country is
fine for up to 30 days a year; longer needs approval from People and legal.

Core hours are 11:00 to 15:00 Eastern, when meetings may be scheduled.
The whole company meets in person twice a year; travel is paid by Bluefin.
