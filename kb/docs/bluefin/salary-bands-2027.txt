Bluefin Analytics — 2027 salary bands (restricted: finance and leadership)
Version 1.0, 2027.

All amounts are base salary in USD for US-based employees. Canada and
Portugal use local bands at 85 and 60 percent of these figures.

- Data Engineer II: 125,000 to 150,000
- Senior Data Engineer: 152,000 to 185,000
- ML Engineer II: 132,000 to 158,000
- Senior ML Engineer: 160,000 to 196,000
- Support Specialist: 62,000 to 78,000

The 2027 merit budget is 4.2 percent of payroll. Equity refreshes are
reviewed once a year, in March.
