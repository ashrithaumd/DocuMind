Bluefin Analytics — security essentials
Version 1.0, 2026.

- Multi-factor authentication everywhere; passkeys are required for
  Snowflake and AWS.
- Customer data never leaves Snowflake except through approved exports.
  Never download customer data to a laptop.
- Access reviews run every quarter; unused access is removed after 60 days.
- Report a suspected incident in #security-incidents or to
  security@bluefin.example within 30 minutes.
- Laptops lock after 10 minutes idle.
- We are SOC 2 Type II certified; the next audit window is March 2027.
