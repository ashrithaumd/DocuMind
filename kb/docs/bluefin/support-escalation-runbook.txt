Support — escalation runbook
Version 1.0, 2026.

First response targets
- Enterprise: 1 hour, around the clock.
- Business: 4 business hours.
- Standard: 1 business day.

When to escalate to engineering
- Wrong numbers in a dashboard that the customer can show us: escalate to
  data-eng as P2 straight away; do not try to fix data by hand.
- Forecasts that look badly wrong for a whole category: escalate to the ML
  on-call.
- Login or SSO problems: check the status page first, then escalate to
  platform.

Refunds and credits up to 500 USD can be approved by a support lead; above
that, finance must approve. Always link the Zendesk ticket in the escalation.
