Tide dashboards — spec notes
Version 1.0, 2026.

Tide is the dashboard product. Each customer gets a dedicated Snowflake
schema and up to 25 dashboards in the standard plan (unlimited in
Enterprise). Data refreshes every 4 hours by default, or hourly in
Enterprise. Dashboards load in under 3 seconds at p90 for datasets up to
500 million rows. Single sign-on through SAML is available in Enterprise
only. Exports: PDF, CSV and scheduled email digests.
